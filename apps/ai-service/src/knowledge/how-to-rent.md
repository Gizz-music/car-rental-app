# How to rent a car

Register or log in, open Cars, pick a start date and an end date, then reserve. The booking covers the half-open range [startDate, endDate): you pay for every night until the return calendar day, and the car is free again on endDate.

The catalog shows price per day. Total price is days × pricePerDay. Adjacent bookings that only touch on the same date do not overlap.

You can see your reservations on the Profile page.
