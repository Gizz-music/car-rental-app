# Fleet and prices

The live catalog on Cars is the source of truth for which models are in stock and what they cost today. Demo cars include compact sedans and SUVs such as Renault Logan, Mazda 3, Toyota Corolla, Honda Civic, Volkswagen Golf, Hyundai Tucson, Kia Sportage, and BMW 3 Series.

Availability depends on the dates you select. Without dates, a car with any current or upcoming booking is shown as unavailable until that booking ends.

Ask about "available cars" or a model name to have the assistant load the current catalog.
